Waabi Driver

Our end-to-end trained system is fully interpretable, and can see exactly why a decision was made. Designed for generalizability, Waabi Driver adapts to new scenarios while maintaining multiple layers of redundancy across both hardware and software. This ensures that if a fault ever occurs, the system defaults to minimal-risk maneuvers.

Waabi World

Our high-fidelity neural simulator is capable of replicating both routine situations and rare edge cases with a high degree of realism.. Efficient, comprehensive testing allows us to launch responsibly and continually improve performance in the highest-risk scenarios.

Mixed Reality Testing

By combining real trucks with real-time synthetic scenarios, we’re unlocking unlimited testing possibilities that were previously impossible to achieve safely or practically. MRT allows us to inject rare and dangerous situations into live operations without putting people or cargo at risk.
