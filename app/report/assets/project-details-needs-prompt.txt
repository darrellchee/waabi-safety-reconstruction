Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: illustration for the fourth slide, "1.4 What is your needs statement?", on an academic project details website.
Primary request: Illustrate independent visitors at Warokutal accessing an elevated, low maintenance emergency supply shelter when a remote road is damaged. Show a compact practical open-sided shelter on piles, shallow pitched roof, stairs and railings, shelves with water containers and sealed food tins, a small storage case and coiled recovery strap.
Scene/backdrop: Simplified remote tropical Australian bushland near Cape York, sparse low trees and grasses, with plenty of uncluttered cream space.
Style/medium: Refined flat editorial illustration with thin dark sage outlines, simple geometric shapes, restrained detail, matte solid fills, no gradients or photorealism. Match a clean existing website diagram system.
Composition/framing: Near-square landscape 8:7 canvas, one cohesive isometric scene centered with generous margins, all objects fully within the frame.
Color palette: Warm cream #f5f6ef background, pale sage #dce5d3 and #b8cbaa, dark olive #53684a outlines, muted blue #9bb9c3 water containers, tan #d7c49b, sparing terracotta #ac653e accents.
Lighting/mood: Calm, practical, self-reliant and reassuring.
Constraints: No words, letters, numbers, captions, logos, watermarks, labels or UI. No Indigenous patterns or symbols. No one driving or walking through floodwater. The illustration is a proposed design concept, not a map, photograph, or existing facility.
