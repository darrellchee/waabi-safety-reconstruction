# Animation validation

This pass uses normal motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`) for reference and local captures. Earlier page screenshots used reduced motion and could establish layout, but could not establish animation fidelity.

## Repeatable protocol

Run from the repository root while the local application is running:

```sh
python scripts/motion_compare.py --side reference
MOTION_LOCAL_URL=http://127.0.0.1:3000 python scripts/motion_compare.py --side local
npx playwright test tests/motion.spec.ts
```

The script tests 1440×900 and 390×844. Playwright drives the public reference browser and the local browser, samples computed styles and bounding boxes during the first five seconds, then visits identical absolute scroll positions forwards and backwards. A separate wheel impulse records native smooth-scroll settling. REA 6.5.0 captures the stable states through CDP; each PNG has a compressed original REA JSON envelope beside it. Numeric traces contain timestamps, scroll positions, text and ancestor opacity/transforms, video state and running animation timings. Exact timeline screenshots cannot be guaranteed by the REA CLI's asynchronous startup; numeric sampling, rather than CLI screenshot completion time, establishes animation progress.

Artifacts are stored in `artifacts/comparison/motion/`. Filenames identify reference/local, route, viewport, operation index and scroll position. `hero-timeline.json.gz` records the initial state, `wheel-*.json.gz` the 600px wheel impulse, and `scroll-*.json.gz` the fixed scroll states. Screenshots are viewport captures rather than full-page captures so sticky positioning remains meaningful.

## Evidence and interpretation

Live video frames are nondeterministic, so pixel differences across the footage do not measure animation accuracy. Compare computed opacity, transforms, clip paths and element bounds first. Reveal triggers must also be compared on a fresh page: once-only reveals intentionally remain visible on the return trip. Scroll-linked transforms should return to the same pose when the scroll position returns.

The reference's initial Home trace at 1440×900 shows the main title at y525 and the introductory copy hidden at scroll0 throughout the sampled initial period. At scroll450 the introductory copy becomes visible while the main title has moved to y75. This contradicts the previous implementation's automatic 1.9s text swap. The reference strength heading and media move at different rates; this is not equivalent to switching strength tabs at arbitrary scroll thresholds. These findings were sent to the implementation agent.

The Company word reveal is once-only: source evidence shows 0.1s linear opacity/color transitions with 0.07s word stagger. A reverse-scroll test must therefore check that revealed words remain readable, rather than resetting their colors. Company hero and quote image parallax are continuous and reversible.

## Limits

A matching sampled transform does not prove every frame is identical. Browser rendering, display refresh rate, video decode and network-dependent hydration can shift live timing. The screenshots and numeric traces establish observed states and expose discrepancies; they do not support a blanket claim of pixel-perfect animation parity.

The observed reference 390px wheel trace for a 600px impulse reaches approximately 479px at 122ms, 570px at 237ms, 594px at 363ms and 600px at 634ms. The reverse impulse reaches 81px at 125ms, 16px at 254ms and zero at 642ms. This supports the source Lenis `lerp: 0.2` behavior. Actual sample timestamps are retained; heavy browser decoding can reduce the sampling frequency below the nominal 100ms.

`early-frames.json.gz` is collected by a read-only requestAnimationFrame probe installed before navigation. It begins when the server-rendered H1 first appears, allowing word opacity and transforms to be observed before DOMContentLoaded returns. `--hero-only` limits a rerun to initial frames, settled hero REA capture and wheel settling; `--route home` or `--route company` limits refinement captures to that page.

The Playwright motion suite covers reversible montage positioning, smooth wheel settling in both directions, once-only reading reveals, portrait rotation with an offscreen pause, and reduced-motion film playback. It is intentionally separate from layout screenshot tests and overrides their reduced-motion default.


## Final production result

The final motion reconstruction passed all 24 Playwright tests, including five explicit normal/reduced-motion checks. Complete normal-motion sweeps of all three pages at desktop/mobile reported no runtime exceptions or horizontal overflow. Fresh REA reference and local captures were repeated after refinement, with overlays and numeric comparisons generated by the script. Montage copy matches the reference at desktop y427.833px and mobile y468.406/468.414px; text tracking and wrapping now agree. Background videos and procedural canvas phases remain independently scheduled.
